# Viral moment detection rubric

Score every candidate moment 1–5 on each signal. A keeper fires **2+ signals at 4+**, with hook strength mandatory.

## The 7 signals

1. **Hook strength** — first 3 seconds stop a scroller. Cold-open on the punch, never on setup ("jadi gini..." is throat-clearing — cut it, start on the claim).
2. **Surprise / controversy** — bold claim, hot take, myth-busting, numbers that sound wrong but are true. Disagreement in comments = reach.
3. **Insight density** — a framework, tactic, or fact the viewer can reuse today. Save-worthy beats like-worthy.
4. **Emotion** — confession, betrayal, grief, triumph, righteous anger. Face + voice crack = retention.
5. **Humor / relatability** — quotable in one line, meme-able, "gila ini gue banget". If it works as a text screenshot, it works as a clip.
6. **Opinion / debate** — takes a side on something the audience already argues about. End the clip on the take, not the walk-back.
7. **Revelation** — behind-the-scenes, money/numbers, "nobody talks about this". Specifics beat generalities ("Rp 800 juta hangus dalam 3 hari" beats "bisnis itu berisiko").

## Hard rules

- **Self-contained or dead.** The clip must land for someone who never saw the podcast. If it needs setup, either the setup fits in the first 3s or the moment is unusable.
- **One idea per clip.** Two good moments in one 90s window → two clips, not one long one.
- **Diversity.** No two clips from the same story/segment. Spread across the episode.
- **Length:** 30–90s sweet spot, 15s floor, 180s ceiling.
- **Trim to the hook.** `start` = the first word of the punch line, minus ~0.3s breathing room. If the good stuff starts at 1:12:04, the clip starts at 1:12:04, not 1:11:50.
- **End on the peak**, not the comedown. Cut right after the laugh / reveal / punch — never let the hosts trail into "ya gitu lah".

## Red flags (reject)

- Inside jokes needing episode context.
- Rambling setup longer than 10s before any payoff.
- Topics already covered by another selected clip.
- Audio garbage (crosstalk, mic failure) — captions can't save it.
- Claims you can't stand behind in the title (no clickbait lies).

## ID + EN specifics

- Keep the original language — never translate speech. A Jawa-English code-switching moment stays as-is; captions transcribe verbatim.
- Titles may be in the clip's dominant language; keep under 60 chars.
